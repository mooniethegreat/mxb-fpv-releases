Kino / KiNO Mod by trbflxr and the Kino team:
https://github.com/trbflxr/kino
The six drone soundbank subsounds in Data/Bundles/drone_win.knd (reference
commit 49f2b8, full revision recorded in assets/audio/PROVENANCE.txt) supply
the motor/air samples embedded in this mod.
They are decoded to mono PCM, with short loop-splice crossfades. The native
RPM/load/speed crossfader is a new implementation, not the original FMOD mix.
Audio remains credited to its original creators. No ownership or license to
the original sounds is claimed by this project.
Kino executable code, CarX, Unity, Rewired and the FMOD runtime are not included.
Drone simulation, hardware abstraction and procedural meshes are new native code.
